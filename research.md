## Topology and geometry of tissue

Cells in a tissue are organized into structures across many scales, from local cellular neighborhoods to whole tumor micro-environments. I study how tools from topology and geometry, such as persistent homology and higher-order relational structures, can encode this spatial organization into graph representations that machine learning models can use. This line of work started with my MSc thesis on topological encodings of tumor micro-environments, and now extends to multi-scale tissue graphs and topological tokens.

I have presented this work in [invited talks](/talks/) at the University of Bern and CHUV.

## Dynamics on spatiotemporal graphs

Disease is not static. I develop generative and state-space models for spatiotemporal graphs that describe how a system moves between interpretable regimes over time, with applications to longitudinal treatment-response prediction.

Key papers: [Baffou, Frossard & Thanou, NeurIPS 2026](/publications/#heron), [Baffou, Depeursinge & Thanou, NeurIPS Workshop 2025](/publications/#regrass)

## Machine learning for clinical decisions

I have also worked on applied machine learning with clinical partners: predicting patients' treatment preferences, identifying imaging biomarkers of disease recurrence in ophthalmology, and building an in-house foundation model for ophthalmology at the Jules-Gonin Eye Hospital.

Key papers: [Starke et al., NEJM AI 2025](/publications/#ethic), [Seiler et al., Scientific Reports 2024](/publications/#oct)
