I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), working in the [LTS4 laboratory](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lts4/) with [Dorina Thanou](https://people.epfl.ch/dorina.thanou) and [Pascal Frossard](https://people.epfl.ch/pascal.frossard). My thesis is on structured representations for spatial biology.

I am interested in how different views and representations of data can be used to extract biological knowledge. In particular, I study how to learn structured representations for tissue and disease modeling across modalities, with an emphasis on spatial data such as spatial omics and radiology.

Before my PhD, I completed a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Data Science at EPFL, where I ranked first in my MSc cohort. Along the way, I worked on machine learning for ophthalmology at the [Jules-Gonin Eye Hospital](https://www.ophtalmique.ch/) and on genomics at [CHUV](https://www.chuv.ch/en/).

Outside the lab, I captain a hip-hop dance team (Swiss champions) and preside over Dés-Astres, an association that uses fiction to talk about real-life topics through live shows and podcasts.
