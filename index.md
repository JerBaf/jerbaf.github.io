I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), working in the [LTS4 laboratory](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lts4/) under the supervision of [Dorina Thanou](https://people.epfl.ch/dorina.thanou) and [Pascal Frossard](https://people.epfl.ch/pascal.frossard). 

My research focuses on developing deep-learning methods to capture the spatial organisation and
interactions of biological systems across scales. I am particularly interested in graph-based and topological approaches that
transform complex, multimodal data into structured representations for computational reasoning and 
human interpretation, and in extending these approaches to model how biological structures and interactions evolve over time

Before my PhD, I completed a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Data Science at EPFL, where I ranked first in my cohort out of 104 students. Alongside my studies, I also had the opportunity to conduct research in hospital environments, exploring how machine learning can be used in real clinical scenarios. It allowed me to interact with clinicians and understand the key challenges they face everyday, while assessing the current gaps existing between research and clinics. This motivated a couple of works we conducted there, such as an in-house foundation models for ophthalmology at the [Jules-Gonin Eye Hospital](https://www.ophtalmique.ch/centre-de-recherche/domaines-de-recherche/imagerie-et-intelligence-artificielle/rio/) and interpretable next-generation sequencing (NGS) pipelines for genomics at [CHUV](https://www.chuv.ch/fr/medecine-precision/accueil/patients-et-familles/medecine-de-precision).

Outside the lab, I captain a hip-hop dance team (current Swiss champions) and preside over Dés-Astres, an association that uses fiction to talk about real-life topics through live shows and podcasts. If you are interested by the latter, do not hesitate to follow our adventures on our [website](https://des-astres.ch/)!
